Formulas & Assumptions

1 square yard = 9 square feet. All internal math is in sq ft; results are shown in sq ft and sq yd. Plot area in sq metres is converted at 1 sq m = 10.7639 sq ft.

Covered area (sq ft) = plot area (sq ft) × (ground coverage% + (floors − 1) × upper coverage%)
Quantity of each material = covered area × consumption norm × (1 + wastage%)
Cost of each material = quantity × rate
Water: tankers = litres ÷ litres per tanker; cost = tankers × tanker rate
Structure subtotal = sum of material costs
Finishes subtotal = covered area × sum of finish rates per sq ft (doors & windows may be a lump sum)
Labour subtotal = covered area × labour rate per sq ft
Services = approval charges + utility charges (lump sums)
Subtotal = structure + finishes + labour + services
Total = subtotal × (1 + contingency%) × (1 + contractor margin%)
Cost per sq ft = total ÷ covered area (sq ft)
Cost per sq yd = cost per sq ft × 9

Notes. Finishes apply only for Standard and A-Class packages (Grey Structure = 0). Package presets are rough estimates and can be overridden. Contingency is applied on the subtotal, then contractor margin on the result. Negative or blank inputs are treated as 0; floors have a minimum of 1 and coverage is capped at 100%.

Sources of defaults. Rates are indicative Karachi market levels for the stated units; norms are common thumb rules for residential RCC frame construction. Replace both with your own supplier quotes and BOQ.
